Yolotl Ramírez in depth

The heart, the hands and trust still taking shape

His name is associated with the heart in Nahuatl. During Tacto Ancestral, filmed on November 10, 2026, Yolotl Ramírez stays close to Fernando. A black standard-size hairless Xoloitzcuintli puppy, he moves his paws and shifts his gaze, offering an opportunity to get to know an individual without rushing to define his character.

An introduction that leaves room for observation

Fernando introduces Yolotl and talks about his black coloring, the hair on top of his head and the calm he perceives that day. That is an impression of the recorded scene, not a diagnosis of lasting temperament or a prediction about other situations.

In one photograph, Yolotl faces the camera with his front paws on a hand. Another portrait shows him against Fernando's chest. These are moments of closeness, not isolated proof of trust.

From paws to back: the touch session

The demonstration begins at the paws and continues across the back and neck. Yolotl shifts his gaze, settles his paws and sometimes lowers his head. Each gesture needs context: stillness or a head turn alone does not establish comfort or discomfort.

Fernando also shows a Xolos Army Naturals balm. Its presence is documented as part of the episode, not turned into veterinary advice or an unverified clinical claim about products for very young puppies.

The encounter comes before the label

Closeness in Tacto Ancestral should never need to be forced. Allowing pauses, observing the whole body and respecting the puppy's limits are central to gentle handling. Fernando explains his interest in gradually familiarizing puppies with human contact.

This recording offers an initial record. Understanding Yolotl's personality will require later moments of play, independent exploration and everyday life, without forcing reactions or treating one scene as his whole story.
