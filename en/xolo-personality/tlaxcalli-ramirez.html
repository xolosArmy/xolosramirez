Tlaxcalli Ramírez in depth

Discovering the world without words: scent, touch and first experiences

Tlaxcalli Ramírez is a black standard-size female Xoloitzcuintli puppy, daughter of Tabasqueña. Fernando introduces her on Café con Xolos Ramírez on November 11, 2026, in a conversation about nonverbal communication. As he speaks, she moves her tail, turns her head and shows the little tuft on her forehead. The video captures an early stage, not an already defined personality.

A very young protagonist

During the introduction, Fernando holds Tlaxcalli and points out the hair on her head. The puppy shifts her gaze and moves her tail. Her portraits show her front paws resting on a hand and the tuft of hair above her forehead.

These gestures can be described, but should not become lasting labels about character. Personality becomes clearer through different experiences, not a single broadcast.

Scent as an early doorway

Fernando discusses the importance of smell for young puppies. As a demonstration, he introduces a lavender balm and brings its scent close. This opens a conversation about olfactory recognition and exploration; it does not prove that Tlaxcalli became relaxed or that the product is safe for every puppy.

With animals this young, aromatic and topical products should not be recommended without veterinary safety guidance. Interest in smells and the effects of a substance are separate questions.

Touch: presence and boundaries

The program then turns to touch. Fernando shows how he supports Tlaxcalli and recalls a story told by Alejandro and Victoria: Mixa Ramírez often curls up near Alejandro while he works.

Mixa's experience belongs to another family and cannot automatically be applied to Tlaxcalli. The scene invites us to respect every puppy's needs and limits, always considering the context.

Learning at the right time

Fernando talks about using rewards later, once puppies are older. Rewarding a behavior to encourage its repetition is more precisely described as operant positive reinforcement; the video does not show Tlaxcalli practicing exercises with treats.

At the end, Fernando also explains the cultural meaning of her name: «tlaxcalli» is a Nahuatl word associated with the tortilla. Language and history frame her introduction without replacing observation of the individual.
