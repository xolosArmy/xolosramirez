XolosNFT, Tonalli Wallet and Xolos Explorer: Real Lineage Use

Nox and Yohualli show how digital records support a real Xoloitzcuintli identity and handover.

A real dog before a token

Nox Ramírez joined his family in Mérida. The breeder's published account links the dog with genealogical paperwork, a microchip and a lineage NFT. The transaction can be inspected. The human story comes first.

Yohualli: documented transfer

The Yohualli handover in Tijuana included a lineage NFT transfer to the new guardian. The token page provides a public reference.

What each tool does

Tonalli Wallet enables self-custody and supported asset operations; Xolos Explorer displays public token and transaction references; the NFT links a digital record with an individual dog. An NFT does not replace a pedigree or independently prove health or ancestry claims.

A continuing Living Lineage

Families can preserve photographs, milestones and stories long after homecoming. Technology matters when it supports that continuity without replacing animal care. Learn about the limits of digital records.
