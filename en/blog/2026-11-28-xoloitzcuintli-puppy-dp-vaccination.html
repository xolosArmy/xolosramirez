Four Xoloitzcuintli puppies receive their first Puppy DP vaccination

On Saturday morning, November 28, 2026, Fernando Ramírez, assisted by Alexandra, documented a health care session at Xolos Ramírez in Mexico City. Four puppies, approximately six and a half weeks old, received their first Puppy DP vaccination and internal deworming.

Four puppies, a new stage of care

Chok Pakal and Nochtli Ramírez were born on October 12, 2026; Tlaxcalli and Tlatoani Ramírez were born on October 13. They were 47 and 46 days old, respectively, on the day of the session. Three of the four puppies had been reserved at the time of recording, while Tlatoani was still available.

The session began with Chok Pakal, followed by Nochtli, a reddish (bermejo) puppy, then Tlaxcalli and finally Tlatoani. The video documents the vaccinations, internal deworming and the recording of vaccine labels in health booklets.

Why ongoing health care matters

Early vaccination is part of a schedule that should continue under veterinary guidance. Puppy DP is used against canine distemper and parvovirus; a first dose does not provide complete protection. Internal deworming is a separate preventive measure and must be tailored to the product, weight, age and health of each puppy.

At Xolos Ramírez, documenting these sessions helps maintain each puppy's health record and share their development with families. This report describes a specific session at the kennel; it is not an administration protocol or a substitute for a veterinarian's instructions.
