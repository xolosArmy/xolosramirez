Tonalli Memo: From Verifiable Stories to a Social Layer

Public eCash memory has real use cases; replies and deeper social interaction are still under development.

A story can have multiple references

The blog carries photographs, context and testimony. Tonalli Memo associates compact messages with public eCash transactions; a known TXID can be inspected. It does not independently prove every narrated claim.

Existing use at Xolos Ramírez

The Weekly Update describes identity, NFTs and community memory. A beginner's guide describes the existing wallet and memo flow.

The social layer is under development

The developing social layer aims to support public replies and connections between conversations and profiles. That is not a claim of a finished social network. Moderation, availability, author identity and indexing remain matters for development and verification.

Memory before artificial engagement

The point is not to generate content for its own sake. It is to preserve meaningful events and let families contribute their perspectives with privacy and consent.
