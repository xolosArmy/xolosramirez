Price and process · Updated September 17, 2026

How much does a registered Xoloitzcuintli from Xolos Ramírez cost?

The short answer is straightforward: the current regular price of a Xoloitzcuintli from Xolos Ramírez is MXN $42,000. The regular price is the same across our Xolos and does not increase because of size, sex, or color. What matters next is understanding what comes with that price, how a reservation is formalized, and which costs depend on the destination.

Regular price

MXN $42,000

Regular price per Xoloitzcuintli from Xolos Ramírez.

Special eCash option

MXN $37,000 equivalent in XEC

This option applies when the full payment is made in eCash (XEC). The exact amount of XEC is calculated at the time the payment is made.

What does “registered” mean here?

Terms such as “high registration” are sometimes used commercially to describe dogs whose breeding, identity, and genealogy are documented. At Xolos Ramírez, the useful distinction is the evidence that accompanies each dog: identification, health follow-up, genealogical documentation when applicable, and a documented history.

The exact documentation may vary according to the puppy's age, the applicable registration, and the destination country. We therefore recommend reviewing the individual dog's documentation before completing a reservation.

What is included in the Xolos Ramírez price?

Health follow-up

Age-appropriate vaccination and deworming, together with the veterinary follow-up completed during raising.

Identification

Microchip identification and the related documentation when applicable to the individual dog's process.

Registration and lineage

FCM/FCI genealogical documentation when applicable and a digital documentary lineage record within the Xolos Ramírez ecosystem.

Ongoing guidance

Guidance about adaptation, feeding, skin care, family life, and questions that may arise after delivery.

Does the price change for Miniature, Intermediate, or Standard Xolos?

No. Xolos Ramírez currently keeps the same regular price of MXN $42,000 across its Xoloitzcuintles, whether Miniature, Intermediate, or Standard. The regular price also does not change because of sex or color.

Availability, delivery timing, and transport logistics can differ from one dog to another.

Is delivery included?

The dog's price and transport logistics are handled separately when the family needs delivery outside the agreed point in Mexico City. National and international delivery depends on the destination, applicable minimum age, route, and required documentation.

Before reserving: request a delivery quote for your city or country. This keeps the dog's price separate from the logistics required for your destination.

How does the reservation process work?

  1. Review the available Xoloitzcuintles and choose the dog you would like to learn more about.
  2. Contact Xolos Ramírez to confirm availability and discuss your lifestyle, location, and expectations.
  3. Have a video call or orientation conversation when appropriate.
  4. If you decide to continue, the current reservation, payment, and delivery terms are confirmed before payment.

Regular price vs. the special XEC option

The main commercial reference remains MXN $42,000. For people who already use eCash, Xolos Ramírez offers a special option to complete the purchase at MXN $37,000 equivalent in XEC, provided that the entire payment is made with XEC.

There is no fixed XEC amount published because the exchange value changes. The exact XEC amount is calculated only when the buyer is ready to complete the payment.
