xolosArmy Community NFTs: Mixa and Onix Family Stories

Community NFTs record family episodes and memories distinct from genealogical lineage.

Lineage and community are different

A canonical lineage NFT is associated with a dog's documented identity. A Community NFT may recognize a photograph, appearance or participation. It does not modify ancestry or replace veterinary records.

Mixa: another digital memory

The Mixa story documents a Community NFT made from a photo used in identification material, along with Tonalli Memo and transfer references. See Mixa's record.

Onix: life after homecoming

Onix appeared with his family on The Xolos Ramírez Show. The story identifies the community mint and a later transfer. The NFT marks the event; family life gives it meaning.

Participation is optional

Families can share stories without a wallet. Those interested in collectibles can consult the Community NFT guide. Consent, privacy, wallet keys and transaction costs matter.
