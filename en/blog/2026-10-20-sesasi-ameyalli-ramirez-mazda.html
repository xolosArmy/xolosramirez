Sesasi and Ameyalli Ramírez: Two Xoloitzcuintli Featured in a Mazda Production in Guadalajara

A delightful update reached the Xolos Ramírez family: Sesasi and Ameyalli appeared in a video production associated with Mazda in Guadalajara, according to Lupita Segundo Cervantes.

From family life to the screen

The story began with a simple conversation. After seeing the two Xoloitzcuintli in a video, we asked where it had been posted. Lupita Segundo Cervantes explained that they appeared in a reel from the Mazda Américas agency in Guadalajara and shared the post so we could document the moment.

Two Xoloitzcuintli, their own stories

At Xolos Ramírez, every dog's story continues long after joining a family. Sesasi and Ameyalli are part of that living memory: everyday photographs, distinctive expressions, and now this on-screen appearance reveal different aspects of their lives with the people who care for them.

A moment worth preserving

Carmen also shared photographs of Sesasi and Ameyalli at home, intimate portraits that contrast with the public setting of a commercial production. We thank Carmen for the photographs and Lupita for sharing the reel and helping us tell the story. This article documents a community milestone; it does not imply that Xolos Ramírez organized or sponsored the production.
