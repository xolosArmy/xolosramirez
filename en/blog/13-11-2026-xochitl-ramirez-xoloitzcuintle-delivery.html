Xóchitl Ramírez: An Ancestral Xoloitzcuintli Lineage Finds a New Home in Guadalajara

Beside the airplane window, Xóchitl Ramírez rides on Alexandra’s lap. She is headed to Guadalajara, where her new home is with a family that already knows what it is like to live with a Xoloitzcuintli.

From Axia’s litter to a family we know

Xóchitl was born at Xolos Ramírez in Mexico City on July 4, 2026. She is a reddish (bermejo) female Xoloitzcuintli of the intermediate size and hairless variety. Her mother is Axia Ramírez and her father is Aztlán Ramírez.

Gustavo Lomelí and his family reserved Xóchitl. They had previously welcomed Sisi, also known as Tomasa, another Xoloitzcuintli from Xolos Ramírez. Xóchitl’s arrival continues the family’s relationship with the kennel.

The flight and arrival in Guadalajara

On Friday, November 13, 2026, Alexandra Ramírez Gutiérrez accompanied Xóchitl from Mexico City to Guadalajara on Aeroméxico flight AM 284, departing from Felipe Ángeles International Airport (AIFA). In Guadalajara, Gustavo’s cousin Rubén Martínez Lomelí met Xóchitl on Gustavo’s behalf, with his authorization.

The bond after the handoff

Responsible breeding extends into daily care and support for the family as their dog settles in. Sharing Xóchitl’s origins and preserving a record of her journey lets that story stay with her family and remain part of the Xoloitzcuintli’s legacy.
