Education and responsible placement

What shapes a Xoloitzcuintli's price, and what does the process include?

If you are considering welcoming a Xoloitzcuintli into your family, one of your first questions may be about cost. A responsible placement is not an online-store transaction: it begins with the individual dog, documented care and a conversation about long-term compatibility.

Guidance for each Xolo depends on factors such as life stage, size, documentation, care already completed and the logistics required for the destination. Rather than publishing an isolated figure, this guide explains what accompanies the process and how to request personalized orientation.

More than a number: documented care and responsible raising

Xolos Ramírez does not focus on volume. Our priorities are welfare, responsible raising and documented continuity for the breed. Each puppy receives veterinary follow-up, age-appropriate nutrition and early social experiences. We also help families consider size, household, expectations and logistics before moving forward.

What does the Xolos Ramírez process include?

The exact documents and health steps depend on age, destination and veterinary timing. The process may include the following components, each documented for the individual Xolo:

🩺

Health certificate

A veterinarian-issued assessment documenting the health findings and care completed before placement.

💉

Vaccination record

Vaccination and deworming appropriate to age and veterinary assessment, accompanied by the corresponding records.

📡

Identification microchip

Identification through an ISO microchip linked to the Xolo’s corresponding documentation.

🔗

Xoloitzcuintli Lineage NFT record

A digital documentary record that accompanies information and evidence about the individual Xolo. It helps preserve a verifiable reference to registered documents and events, but it does not replace FCM/FCI registration, genetic testing or veterinary assessment.

What to compare before making a decision

A responsible decision considers documented health, raising conditions, socialization, clarity about records and support during adaptation. Compare processes and ask every necessary question before deciding whether a particular Xolo may fit your family.
