On November 3, 2026, Xilonen Ramírez began a new chapter. She met Nidea, the sister of Marcelo de la Torre, at Tim Hortons in Plaza Encuentro Fortuna in northern Mexico City. Nidea received Xilonen in person, and photographs documented their meeting.

An afternoon that opens a new story

In the delivery photographs, Nidea gently holds Xilonen. It is a simple, personal moment: the close of her early chapter with Xolos Ramírez and the beginning of her life with Marcelo’s family.

The handover was coordinated through conversations and a video call. The meeting took place in Mexico; a possible onward journey to Ireland was being considered for mid-January 2027, with no confirmed flight or travel date at the time of writing.
