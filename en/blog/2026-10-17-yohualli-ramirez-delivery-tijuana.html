Yohualli Ramirez’s Delivery in Tijuana

A trip from Mexico City to close one chapter of her upbringing and begin a new family story, supported by physical documentation and a digital lineage record.

Published: October 17, 2026 · Category: Deliveries · Digital lineage

On the morning of October 17, 2026, Yohualli Ramirez arrived in Tijuana to meet her new guardian, Edgar Vasquez. Fernando Ramirez traveled with her from Mexico City on Aeromexico flight AM 170 to personally complete the handoff and accompany the final documentation steps.

The meeting took place at the domestic arrivals area of Tijuana International Airport. After weeks of coordination, veterinary preparation and document organization, Yohualli finally joined her new family.

Two moments from the same delivery

The handoff was documented in two Shorts published on the Xolos Ramírez YouTube channel: the meeting in Tijuana and Yohualli’s first moments with her new family.

Her paperwork continues with her new family

Yohualli was delivered together with the paperwork prepared so Edgar can continue the corresponding process with the Federación Canófila Mexicana (FCM). The package included the original breeding certificate and a signed power of attorney from the breeders, together with the identification copies needed to support that authorization.

The purpose is to allow her new guardian to complete the relevant administrative process and obtain Yohualli’s individual registration in his name, subject to FCM requirements. The physical documentation remains the formal basis of the kennel-club process.

Living Lineage: from physical records to on-chain memory

Before the delivery, Yohualli also received her official Lineage NFT within the Xolos Ramírez collection. The NFT is a complementary digital layer for identity and provenance. It does not replace FCM documents; instead, it preserves a public, verifiable reference to her history within the Living Lineage.

A story that continues across the border

The Tijuana handoff is not the end of Yohualli’s record; it is the moment her story moves into a new home. From now on, her everyday life will continue with Edgar and his family, while Xolos Ramírez preserves the memory of her origin, lineage and journey to this meeting.

At Xolos Ramírez, every delivery brings together three elements: the human relationship with a new family, the documents that establish the dog’s identity, and a digital memory that can remain verifiable over time.
