From Firma Wallet to Tonalli and from FIRMA to XEC: an end-to-end tutorial

This guide turns a real eCash mainnet test into a reproducible learning path: back up a Firma Wallet seed, recover it in Tonalli, discover Firma Alpha under the eCash/Cashtab 1899 profile, and redeem FIRMA for XEC through an Agora offer signed locally.

Rule number one: your 12 words never leave your devices.

Do not paste the seed into ChatGPT, Telegram, email, GitHub, web forms, screenshots or support messages. Learn with small amounts. If a seed is exposed, treat it as compromised.

The funding rail and the on-chain asset are separate concepts.

Firma may expose funding methods involving stablecoins or other external rails. That does not mean every balance shown in the app is Firma Alpha. Firma Alpha, Firma USD, fCHF and fEUR should not be treated as the same token. To follow the on-chain portion of this guide, verify that the asset is Firma Alpha (FIRMA).

Before you begin

  • Set up Firma Wallet.
  • Have access to Tonalli Wallet at app.tonalli.cash or the instance you use.
  • Back up the Firma Wallet seed offline.
  • Start with an amount you are comfortable using as a test.
  • Keep a small amount of pure XEC available for transaction fees.

Create or open Firma Wallet and back up the seed

Write the 12 words down in their exact order. Do not photograph them. The mnemonic is the cryptographic root of the wallet: restoring it in Tonalli does not “send” a balance between apps. It lets another wallet derive the same eCash keys.

Fund Firma using the method supported by the current app

Firma may offer fiat or stablecoin funding and, in some versions, a Solana-wallet connection for assets such as USDC, USDT or EURC. Follow the current app flow and complete any verification requirements that apply.

Do not continue based on a name or ticker alone. Verify which on-chain asset ended up under your eCash seed.

Verify that you actually hold Firma Alpha

The Firma Alpha supported by Tonalli has this canonical identity:

Token ID: 0387947fd575db4fb19a3e322f635dec37fd192b5941625b66bc4b2c3008cbf0
Protocol: ALP standard · type 0
Decimals: 4

If an app or explorer shows a different Token ID, do not assume it is the same asset.

Restore the same 12 words in Tonalli

Choose wallet restore/import and enter the same 12 words directly on your device. For a Firma/Cashtab-compatible seed, Tonalli should use:

eCash / Cashtab
BIP44 coin type: 1899
Base identity: m/44'/1899'/0'/0/0

If you are recovering an old Tonalli wallet created before this compatibility layer, use Tonalli Legacy 899. Do not use Legacy 899 for a Firma/Cashtab seed merely because “899” looks familiar.

Run an extended rescan

The balance may live at a different child address within the same HD tree. Use Extended rescan. Tonalli scans receive/change branches for the active profile and aggregates UTXOs controlled by that seed.

A useful cross-check is to restore the same seed in Cashtab in a controlled environment and verify that both clients discover the same FIRMA balance. The interfaces do not necessarily have to display the same “next receive address” at a given moment for the UTXOs to belong to the same HD tree.

Add pure XEC for dust and fees if needed

A UTXO containing FIRMA also carries sats, but those sats are attached to the token output. Tonalli separates pure spendable XEC from sats attached to token UTXOs. If the wallet reports insufficient pure XEC, send a small amount of XEC to the wallet's canonical eCash address and wait for it to become spendable.

Optional: first test a small FIRMA send

Under Send Firma Alpha, send a small amount to an eCash address or .xec alias. Tonalli previews the transaction, refreshes state, and only then derives the signatories for the exact HD paths that own the selected inputs.

Open the DEX and choose “Redeem Firma to XEC”

In the XEC ↔ FIRMA market, choose Redeem Firma to XEC, enter the amount and press Preview operation. Nothing is signed during preview.

Review five key values: FIRMA to lock, expected XEC, effective price, official bid and estimated fee. Tonalli also checks sweeper capacity before the offer is created.

Confirm, sign locally and broadcast

At confirmation time, Tonalli rebuilds the plan from fresh state. It queries the redemption bid again; if the bid changed, the wallet requires a new preview. If all checks match, Tonalli signs locally and broadcasts an on-chain Agora offer.

This is not a bank withdrawal and Tonalli does not take custody. FIRMA is locked by the offer covenant until the sweeper accepts it. Once accepted, XEC arrives and the wallet balance updates.

What is actually happening

Firma Wallet, Cashtab and Tonalli can operate on the same eCash identity when they share the same seed and derivation scheme. Restoring a seed does not move funds; the UTXOs remain on the blockchain. A different interface simply derives the keys that control them.

FIRMA → XEC redemption is a second step: Tonalli creates an Agora offer at the current redemption bid. The sweeper accepts that offer and settlement happens on-chain.

Common problems

  • “I restored the seed but see no balance”: verify eCash/Cashtab 1899 and run an extended rescan.
  • “I see FIRMA but cannot send it”: check for pure spendable XEC to pay dust and fees.
  • “The bid/oracle is unavailable”: do not bypass the protection; wait and generate a fresh preview later.
  • “The bid changed”: this is normal protection; generate a new preview.
  • “My old Tonalli wallet looks empty”: use Tonalli Legacy 899, which reproduces the historical derivation engine.

What this tutorial does not promise

This funnel ends in self-custodied XEC. It is not, by itself, a withdrawal to pesos, dollars or a bank account. Funding methods, KYC, stablecoin, Solana and other external rails belong to the services that provide them and may change.

Also keep Firma Alpha distinct from other Firma-branded assets. Before moving value, always verify Token ID, network, amount, destination and preview.

Summary

Fund Firma → verify Firma Alpha → back up seed → restore in Tonalli with 1899 → extended rescan → fund pure XEC for fees → preview redemption → fresh-state revalidation → local signature → Agora offer → sweeper → XEC
