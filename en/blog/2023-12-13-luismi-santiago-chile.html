Luismi: a Mexican Xoloitzcuintli in Santiago, Chile

Luismi Ramírez’s arrival at Santiago airport in Chile and subsequent family videos with Pedrito and Vika: a documented Xolos Ramírez story.

Original Jimdo account published: 2023-12-13

The account of Luismi Ramírez published on December 13, 2023 describes his family receiving him at the airport in Santiago, Chile. It is one of the documented international stories of Xolos Ramírez.

The original describes family videos of Luismi playing at home with Pedrito, a German Shepherd. Vika is also named in an image caption. These everyday scenes extend his story beyond the airport arrival.

The source identifies Santiago, Chile and the airport reception, but does not establish the exact handoff date. The date shown here refers to the original publication.
