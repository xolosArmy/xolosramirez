What does “miniature Xoloitzcuintli” mean?

The miniature variety is the most compact recognized size of the Xoloitzcuintli. It is not a separate “toy” breed. A miniature Xolo keeps the breed's intelligence, close family bond, socialization needs, and distinctive character in a smaller physical format.

For families living in apartments, traveling often, or simply preferring a smaller dog, the miniature size can be practical. It still requires exercise, training, companionship, and appropriate skin care.
