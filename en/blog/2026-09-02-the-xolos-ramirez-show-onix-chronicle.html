Onix Ramirez in The Xolos Ramirez Show: from a shy first arrival to family life in Texas

Onix Ramirez had already crossed an important threshold when he left Xolos Ramirez and joined Adrian Castellanos and his wife in Texas. The episode of The Xolos Ramirez Show published on August 28, 2026 documents what came next: not the delivery itself, but the everyday process through which a young Xoloitzcuintli becomes part of a household.

In the conversation, Adrian and his wife describe Onix as initially shy during his first days at home. That reserve did not last long. As he became familiar with the space and the people around him, his puppy energy began to appear more clearly, along with a strong emotional attachment to the family.

A bond built in ordinary moments

One of the details that stands out is how quickly Onix became physically and emotionally close to his family. They explain that he has slept with them since his first nights at home and that much of his communication happens through eye contact and body language. Those small routines have become part of the way they understand his moods, needs and intentions.

The episode offers a practical view of a Xoloitzcuintli growing inside a family rather than an abstract description of the breed. Onix is energetic, affectionate and increasingly expressive, and his personality continues to become more defined as he matures.

Skin care, weather and daily routines

Adrian and his wife also discuss the practical care required by a hairless Xoloitzcuintli. Their routine includes paying attention to Onix's skin, using clothing when temperatures are low and allowing him to enjoy time in the sun. The discussion presents these habits as part of normal family life rather than as isolated maintenance tasks.

These daily decisions are also part of learning the individual dog. The family adjusts care according to weather, activity and Onix's own preferences as they continue to understand what makes him comfortable.

Socialization, play and positive reinforcement

The family describes his socialization with people and other animals, including the cat in the household. Their experience shows a gradual process of learning boundaries, building familiarity and finding ways to coexist.

Play and training are another recurring part of the episode. Adrian and his wife use positive reinforcement as they teach and guide Onix, while also recognizing the intelligence and sensitivity that they see in him. The result is a picture of training as communication rather than simple obedience.

A Mexican ancestral breed living in Texas

For Onix's family, living with him also carries a cultural dimension. They are sharing daily life in Texas with a breed deeply connected to Mexican history, and the episode becomes part of that continuity: an ancestral dog, a contemporary family and a story that crosses borders without losing its origin.
