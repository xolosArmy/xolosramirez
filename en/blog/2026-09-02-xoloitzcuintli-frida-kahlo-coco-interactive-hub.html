Medium common dog with a full coat; guardian, hunter, and companion.

2. Paleogenomics and Canine Ectodermal Dysplasia (FOXI3)

4. Pop Culture, Gentrification, and Constitutional Protection

The report analyzes the media impact of Coco, the Xolo as a symbol for Club Tijuana, the transformation of its status, and constitutional animal-protection reforms.

From marginalized dog to luxury symbol

In Roma Norte and Condesa, the report describes the Xoloitzcuintli as a status symbol and places documented puppies at roughly $2,500–$4,000 USD in prestige markets.

The “Coco Effect” and Dante

Dante reflects hairlessness and the hanging tongue associated with oligodontia; as an alebrije, he recovers the psychopomp role for a global audience.

Constitutional Reform · Article 73

The report notes that animal protection was elevated to constitutional status and links the reform to a stricter framework against irresponsible backyard breeding.
