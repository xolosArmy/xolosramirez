How to Choose a Xoloitzcuintli Puppy: Size, Personality & Family Fit

October 25, 2026 · Editorial content by Xolos Ramírez

Choosing a Xoloitzcuintli is more than finding an available puppy. The goal is to discover an individual dog whose needs, personality and development fit the life you can offer.

Begin with your everyday life

Before comparing photos, think about an ordinary weekday. Where will your dog sleep? Who will spend time with them? How will your family handle training, socialization, exercise and veterinary appointments? Include the people and other pets already sharing your home.

Discuss responsibilities early: feeding, travel arrangements, time alone, and skin care when relevant to a hairless Xolo. Excitement matters, but a realistic plan makes room for a lasting relationship.

Consider size, coat variety and age

Xoloitzcuintli dogs come in different sizes and both coated and hairless varieties. Any expected adult size is an estimate based on available information, not a guarantee. Ask about the individual puppy and their parents rather than relying on a label alone.

A newborn offers the experience of following a puppy's growth almost from the beginning. An older puppy may already display more observable behavior. Neither option is universally better: the right choice depends on the dog, their care and your household.

For more context, see the Xoloitzcuintli size guide.

Look beyond appearance

A photo, a rare-looking color or a particular expression may spark an immediate connection. That can be the beginning of your choice, but it helps to watch recent videos and ask about play, curiosity, rest and human interaction.

Puppy behavior evolves with age and environment. A short description is not a promise of adult temperament. Ask what caretakers have actually observed, and under what circumstances.

Why having a choice matters

A varied and clearly presented selection gives families a chance to compare priorities: expected size, sex, appearance, stage of development and practical timing. The experience should make informed choice easier, not create artificial urgency.

At Xolos Ramírez, individual profiles help distinguish dogs that are available from those already reserved. The aim is to connect a family with a particular Xolo, not to treat puppies as interchangeable.

Five questions to ask before reserving

  • What size and care needs fit my household?
  • What is confirmed about this puppy's age, health and development?
  • Can I see recent photos or video and ask about their behavior?
  • Which documents and follow-up support apply to this puppy?
  • What are the reservation, payment and delivery terms?

A video call can help you learn more and ask specific questions. If no puppy feels right yet, there is no need to force the decision.
