How does the process begin?

1. Meet the profiles. Review each Xoloitzcuintle’s age, size, personality, and needs.

2. Write to us by WhatsApp or email. Tell us what you are looking for and where you are located.

3. We talk about your family. We review location, lifestyle, and compatibility; when there is a good match, we personally explain the next steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the adoption include?

It includes a vaccination card, microchip, care guide, and post-adoption follow-up.

Specific conditions are explained personally after confirming availability, compatibility, and the family’s location.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we can manage international shipping with the necessary veterinary and travel requirements.
