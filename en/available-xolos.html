Before taking the next step

1. Meet the profiles. Review each Xoloitzcuintle’s age, size, personality, and needs.

2. Write to us by WhatsApp or email. Tell us what you are looking for and where you are located.

3. We talk about your family. We review location, lifestyle, and compatibility; when there is a good match, we personally explain the next steps.
