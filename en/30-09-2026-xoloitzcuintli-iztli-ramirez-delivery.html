Iztli Ramirez’s Delivery: A New Chapter for Oce in Mexico City

September 30, 2026 · Xolos Ramírez

Home / Blog / Iztli Ramirez

On September 30, 2026, Iztli Ramirez was delivered to his new guardian, Jeffrey Nelson, at Tim Hortons in Plaza Encuentro Fortuna, Mexico City. Jeffrey travelled to the capital to receive him. This chronicle preserves the meeting and the documentation accompanying a new chapter in Iztli’s story.

Iztli, previously known as Oce

Iztli was born on July 14, 2026. He is a black male in the small intermediate size category, a son of Pitágoras Ramirez and Reyna Ramirez. During his reservation he was known as Oce; that name remains a historical reference linking earlier records to the same dog.

His story already has a place in the Xolos Ramírez archive. The Puppy DP vaccination chronicle (Spanish) and his Personalidad Xolo session (Spanish) document earlier stages of his growth.

Preparation and health records

Preparation included identification through ISO microchip 900255002683022. Iztli received his rabies vaccination on September 29, 2026, and his health certificate was issued that afternoon. These dates preserve a specific record of the preparation before delivery.

The meeting at Plaza Encuentro Fortuna

The handover to Jeffrey took place on September 30 at Tim Hortons in Plaza Encuentro Fortuna. The transaction was paid in full, for MXN $42,000.

During the meeting, Jeffrey said he was considering remaining in Mexico City with his girlfriend. This is recorded as a plan he expressed at the time, rather than confirmation of a permanent home address.

Digital lineage and memory on eCash

Iztli’s lineage NFT was minted on September 30, 2026 and subsequently transferred to his new guardian. This digital record accompanies his story and makes the NFT’s history accessible alongside his documentation.

The NFT reference is 6a14c61eee376cb86ec310bc0a9bf68ed501439ac3ab0378c1a9885e559165ed. Tonalli Memo preserves the on-chain memory of the mint in transaction 15eb78d23807a5d0a73b6e4fca0963fc8677bcb298ad29d0060e73386eb5d460. The subsequent transfer is recorded in 62320a94970049c5242fa642aca524ec3d1b710ea232d8b78e2c497b0ea1a3f2.

These references identify the lineage record and distinguish its creation from the later transfer. Digital memory becomes part of Iztli’s archive, alongside his health records, photographs and videos.

Support continues after delivery

For Xolos Ramírez, delivery begins a new stage of support: guidance on care, settling in and daily life, and continuity in the dog’s story with his family. Iztli leaves the available catalogue, while this permanent page preserves his delivery and links to his earlier history.
