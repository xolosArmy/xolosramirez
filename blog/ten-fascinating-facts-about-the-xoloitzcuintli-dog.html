One Breed, Three Sizes

Unlike many ancient breeds, the Xoloitzcuintli developed naturally in three distinct sizes, recognized globally for their elegant, sturdy build.

Standard

The original size used for hunting and protection. Sturdy and athletic, standing up to 23 inches tall.

Miniature

A versatile companion size, perfect for semi-active households while maintaining the breed's protective instincts.

Toy

The smallest variety, historically favored as 'living heating pads' for the elderly or ill.

Ancient Mythology

The God Xolotl and the Journey to Mictlán

The name Xoloitzcuintli comes from the Aztec god Xolotl (the god of fire and lightning) and Itzcuintli (dog). According to legend, Xolotl created the dog from a sliver of the Bone of Life to protect the living.

Aztecs believed these dogs guided the souls of the deceased through the treacherous journey to Mictlán (the underworld). Without a Xolo by their side, a soul could remain lost in darkness forever.
